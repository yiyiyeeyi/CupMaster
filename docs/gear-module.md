# Equip Module

**Gear Guide** is editorial/product guidance for learning, comparison, beginner setups, and compatibility. **My Gear** is the user's actual equipment and calibration data. They may link but must never collapse into one record type.

Experience can lead to Gear Guide, then optionally My Gear, recipe compatibility, and Quick Prepare auto-fill. Empty My Gear is valid. Recipe defaults always permit Start Brew; gear suggestions explain tradeoffs without becoming prerequisites.
